About HBR

15 years of building trust with buyers worldwide — factory-direct from Dongguan, China.

Back to Home

HBR Electronics (洪柏荣电子) is a factory-direct exporter of mobile phone spare parts and accessories, established in 2009 in Shenzhen and relocated to Dongguan in 2017. We specialise in LCD screens, cover glass, back housing and batteries for iPhone and major Android brands — supplying wholesale and B2B buyers across Europe, the Americas, Southeast Asia, the Middle East and Africa.

With over 15 years in the industry and a dedicated quality-control process at every production stage, HBR has grown to serve 500+ clients in 50+ countries. Our location in Dongguan — minutes from Shenzhen port — gives us a distinct logistics advantage for competitive pricing and fast worldwide delivery.
